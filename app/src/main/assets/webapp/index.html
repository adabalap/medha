Brief the home-screen digest, via /widget/content

    

      Samsung's Now Brief has no third-party API — its custom cards are
      written by you in Settings and generated by Samsung's own AI, with no field an app can
      fill. So Medha has its own brief instead: a home-screen widget every paired app can
      write to at once. Each app owns a section, sections expire after 24 hours by
      default, and one noisy app cannot crowd the others out.
      Add the Medha widget to your home screen once and anything posted here
      appears on it.
